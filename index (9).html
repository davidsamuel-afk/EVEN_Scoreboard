CSV Schema Reference
Every row = one agent. Both payments and activations can be in the same file.
ColumnRequired?Description
agent_nameREQUIREDFull name · Priya Sharma
zoneREQUIREDNorth / South / West
roleREQUIREDIS (Inside Sales) or RM (Relationship Manager)
paymentsREQUIRED DailyDaily payment count · 14
activationsREQUIRED WeeklyWeekly activation count · 52
photo_urlOPTIONALUse https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/FILE_ID for Google Drive
Optional zone-total override columns:
(blank header)OPTIONALLeave one blank column as separator
(blank) / IS / RM / TOTALOPTIONALRow label — describes which total this row contains
NORTHOPTIONALOverride total for North zone
SOUTHOPTIONALOverride total for South zone
WESTOPTIONALOverride total for West zone
